# 0.81: TINEPACK6 (HEC) against TINEPACK5

`scripts/bench/tinepack6.py` (defaults: 40 runs x 30 steps), raw numbers in
`0.81-tinepack6.json`. Corpora are generated by the repository's own writers:
`text` is the TINEPACK5 bench corpus (recorded runs with growing histories),
`mixed` adds random binary blobs (about a quarter of the raw bytes).

Hardware: 4 vCPU Intel Xeon @ 2.10 GHz (shared cloud VM, load ~0.6), Python
3.13.18, `backports.zstd`, pure-Python HEC engine. Times are best of 3.

| Corpus | Raw bytes | TINEPACK5 | TINEPACK5 slim | TINEPACK6 (kept) | v5 / v6 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| text | 14,820,493 | 130,180 | 117,255 | 38,823 (`hec`) | 3.35x |
| mixed | 19,738,993 | 5,051,349 | 5,038,478 | 4,957,526 (`split`) | 1.02x |

TINEPACK6 candidates on `mixed`: all-HEC 4,979,724, split 4,957,526, plain
5,050,086; the writer keeps the smallest, so random binary bodies cost what
zstd costs and the text around them still shrinks.

| Corpus | Encode v5 | Encode v6 | Decode v5 | Decode v6 (MB/s) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| text | 157.6 | 13.1 | 41.6 | 38.3 |
| mixed | 148.7 | 6.5 | 51.3 | 47.0 |

Decode includes every id, envelope and boundary check of `inspect_pack`.
The encoder is the pure-Python reference (it tries up to three candidates and
verifies its own output), roughly 10-20x slower than TINEPACK5: this is why
TINEPACK6 is opt-in on the wire. Sizes are on generated corpora, not recorded
user data; expect different ratios on real repositories.
